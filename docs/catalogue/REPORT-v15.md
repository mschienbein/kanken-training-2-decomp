The old 5.04 `_snputc.o` mismatch remains a real negative. The newly inspected
5.06-b960 definition is a different positive input, not a relaxed comparison of
that failed body. The licensed archive is
`.cache/toolchain/armcc/5.06/b960/lib/armlib/c_5.l`, SHA-256
`00214991a43ef47f201fba5b54da0a7f509baf954c2fbd6ea9ebf2ef151e1c08`.

* `_snputc.o`, ar-header offset **753252**, member SHA-256
  `fa2a43ecd529175c1aeaae66c040c7b3f5655526e6b90ebf04751b0ff555853a`:
  one complete 16-byte executable, nonwritable `.text` section, alignment 2,
  `$t` throughout, global Thumb-tagged `STT_FUNC _snputc`, exact size 16,
  **zero relocations**, no borrowed tail or pool. Its complete original/object
  hash is `1fb4464921189a4fc75aa39ff4ef5232b71f69bd07db69ced34f4bc173dbf4cc`.
* `__0snprintf.o`, offset **529884**, member SHA-256
  `2690e0808b31373fd977cc76f497ff8696ea0902ceab9388677f995723a8ce88`:
  the whole 60-byte section reencodes exactly at `00100360`, including all three
  actual relocations. Its sized declaration is **54**, not 60; mapping changes
  to `$d` at 54, and the existing owner/pool/padding records are not extended.
* Parent entry placement is independent of the callback field: the unchanged
  208-byte caller at `00136510` has its retained actual direct call at `001365b8`
  to `00100360` in Thumb state. The generator binds the whole caller record/body,
  the parent's whole record and the actual call decoding.
* The parent's two direct named imports, at offsets **26** and **40**, target the
  independently classified complete owners `_printf_char_common` at `001005ca`
  and `_sputc` at `001005f0`. Their identities, ISA, complete records, whole-body

  `_snputc`, with addend **34** and Thumb tagging. Static ELF arithmetic exactly
