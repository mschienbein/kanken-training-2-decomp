### Why R2-01 is not an arbitrary layout preference

The [ELF section-header contract](https://gabi.xinuos.com/elf/03-sheader.html#section-header-table-entry) states that `sh_addr` must be congruent to zero modulo `sh_addralign`. The real input metadata is 8, not an inferred C-type assumption. `.data` is writable/non-mergeable and its bytes exist at the tested address; overlap checks and byte equality alone cannot satisfy its address constraint. A new recorded denominator also cannot waive a compiler object's alignment requirement.

Likewise, [ELF COMDAT rules](https://gabi.xinuos.com/elf/03-sheader.html#section-groups) distinguish discarding duplicate groups of one signature from coalescing unrelated objects. The C++ two-TU fixture's actual group contents are `0100000007000000` (COMDAT flag, member index 7). No Nintendo data, assembler, mock compiler or byte-blob source is used in either real-compiler failure fixture.
